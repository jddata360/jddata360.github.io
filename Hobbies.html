Beyond the spreadsheets and supply chains, here's what keeps life interesting — solving puzzles at speed, painting tiny worlds, and making music on instruments that fit in a bag.

🟧 Speed Cubing

Solving the Rubik's Cube as fast as possible — a blend of pattern recognition, muscle memory, and calm under pressure. Great training for analytical thinking!

🎨 Mini Canvas Art

Small canvases, big expression. I enjoy painting intricate miniature artworks — it's meditative and a wonderful counterbalance to data-heavy work.

🎵 Instrumentalist

Playing the Kalimba (thumb piano) and Ukelele — two instruments that are portable, joyful, and endlessly expressive. Music is my way of switching off and recharging.
